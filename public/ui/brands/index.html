Getting started · four steps from zero to a takedown · how it works →
  1. Add a brand below — its name and official domains. Everything else is optional.
  2. On the brand page press START SCAN. The official site is fingerprinted, look-alikes are discovered and each one is visited and scored. You follow every step live.
  3. Open Findings: review the evidence and confirm real phishing or dismiss the rest. Known-legit domains can be removed on the Watchlist.
  4. From a confirmed finding: investigate the site & its network, map the attack infrastructure, generate the evidence package and draft the takedown.
