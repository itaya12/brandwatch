Finds the fake sites impersonating your brands — and gets them taken down.

BrandWatch finds impostors of your brand on the web and on social media — look-alike websites, fake Facebook pages and Instagram profiles that use the brand's name (an AI reads what they post), and ads that sell fakes of its products. It visits each site in a sandbox, scores and explains everything, maps the attacker's infrastructure and drafts the takedown, including the report to Meta. Nothing is sent automatically: an analyst decides every step.
